Horizon Cast

Show this computer's screen on your Fire TV. Nothing to install.

On the TV, open Horizon's AirPlay tile and choose Cast from Computer.
  1. The TV and this computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Choose Share screen, then pick Entire screen (or one window).
  3. To stop, press Stop here or Back on the TV remote.

Sound comes along from Chrome and Edge (tick "Share system audio"). Firefox and Zen send the picture only.
